The two problems

  • parser (issue): the shared parser reads every date month first, and our tenants write the day first. A booking for 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the large one, and the queued fix covers only this one.
  • hint (issue): the booking-v3 form prints a month-first hint under the date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. The date is swapped here too, but the fix is in the form, not in the parser.

Why one ticket hid them

The cause depends on the tenant: where they are, and which booking form they run. The intake tag is only a guess from the reporter's browser, and the table shows how often it names the other problem. Merging by subject line (decision 0001) could not tell the two apart: the subjects match, the causes do not.

Some reports read like a one-day move. The desk counts them where they are filed, under their tenant's cause, and footnotes them. They do not make a third problem.

Lessons

  • Count by cause, not by subject. The tenant and the form tell you the cause; the subject and the intake tag do not.
  • Count a report once, where it is filed, and footnote how it reads.
